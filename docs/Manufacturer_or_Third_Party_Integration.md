# Manufacturer and third-party components

SkyRelief was assembled from commercial parts. Buying a part gives no right to redistribute its maker's CAD, drawings or artwork, and this repository includes none.

| Component (family) | Used on | Source of design | What this repository provides |
|---|---|---|---|
| F450-class frame and PDB | Delivery | Commercial kit, maker not recorded | Name, listed mass, replacement quote P11. No geometry (D01–D04 withheld) |
| 2212 920 KV motors, 1045 props, 30 A ESCs | Delivery | Commercial | Names, quotes P06–P08 |
| Pixhawk 2.4.8 | Delivery | Commercial board in the Pixhawk family; firmware ArduPilot (GPLv3, not redistributed) | Name, quote P01, role in [electronics repo](https://github.com/Aarav-J-10/SkyRelief-Electronics-Integration-Repository) |
| NEO-M8N, FS-iA6B, FS-i6, 3DR telemetry, UBEC, buzzer, safety switch | Delivery | Commercial | Names, quotes where found |
| MG90S-marked servo | Delivery latch | Commercial (TowerPro-marked) | Photo `pld20`, quote P05 |
| Carbon scout frame | Scout | Commercial, maker not recorded | Name only |
| GHF411 PRO-BMI AIO, FT Gremlin 1104, R81, Zorro | Scout | Commercial; firmware Betaflight (GPLv3, not redistributed) | Names, quotes P12–P16 |
| Caddx-labelled FPV, Emax goggles | Both | Commercial | Names only |

Vendor and tutorial diagrams found in the historical archive (for example a JHEMCU wiring illustration and a motor data graphic) are excluded and cited only by name in the source register. Trademarks identify equipment and imply no endorsement.
