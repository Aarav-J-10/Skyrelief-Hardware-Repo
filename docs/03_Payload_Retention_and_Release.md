# Payload retention and release

**Historical SkyRelief hardware dossier with modern engineering reconstruction.** Source-supported components and connectivity are distinguished from new reference implementations. Unrecovered pin assignments, firmware parameters and code must not be treated as as-built records or electrically validated flight instructions.

The [functional sequence](../drawings/release_functional_diagram.svg) is RETAIN -> SERVO ACTUATION -> RETAINING-MEMBER WITHDRAWAL -> PAYLOAD SEPARATION. The photographed latch in pld20 supports a servo-operated release description. Its MG90S marking and the sketch's Tower Pro 9g label do not establish identical exact authentic devices.

Keep four layers distinct: the verbal functional mechanism, D05 fixed support, the photographed assembly and the independently authored R01 bench gate. R01 uses new dimensions: 6 mm neck, 10 mm coupon flange, 14 mm release aperture and 18 mm translation. The flange is geometrically retained over the neck and fits through the aligned aperture. These are not historical measurements.

The gate has nominal 0.5 mm rail clearances, unqualified for any process. Three part solids and two assembly states are exported. Nineteen sampled translations are checked for chassis/gate common volume. This does not establish continuous swept clearance, load-bearing capacity, wear, binding under force or release reliability. No servo, linkage, mechanical stop or output command is selected.
