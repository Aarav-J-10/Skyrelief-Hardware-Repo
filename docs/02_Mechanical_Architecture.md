# Mechanical architecture

**Historical SkyRelief hardware dossier with modern engineering reconstruction.** Source-supported components and connectivity are distinguished from new reference implementations. Unrecovered pin assignments, firmware parameters and code must not be treated as as-built records or electrically validated flight instructions.

The frame topology comprises two central plates, four arms and four motor positions, broadly consistent with pld25. D02 imports as 30 solids: two plate-like, four arm-like and 24 fastener solids. STEP product labels include M3 x 12 and M3 x 5; the written inventory lists sixteen M3 x 10 screws and forty-eight washers. This discrepancy remains unresolved.

D01/D03 are separate plate sources, D04 an arm, D05 one fixed bracket/support. D05 contains no servo, horn, moving retaining member, kinematic joint or actuation definition. None of these frame sources contains the complete propulsion, battery, avionics or payload installation. Source export coordinates are not automatically aircraft forward/up.

The fresh [numerical audit](../data/source_geometry_audit.csv) records import, bounds, valid BRep, closed shells and positive volumes. Plate/arm common volumes remain in the frame model; no interference-free assembly is claimed. Commodity-style F450 geometry does not establish installed revision, material or ownership. Photographs cannot establish fabrication tolerances or CAD equivalence.
