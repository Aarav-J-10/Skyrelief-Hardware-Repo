Landing gear, vibration base, power module, folding GPS base, safety switch, buzzer, FS-i6 transmitter, delivery FPV assembly, pack checker, fasteners, FPV goggles, range sensor, scout frame, scout pack, scout FPV, scout harness, ground station and charging equipment. Unpriced means unknown, not free.

## R01 reference

R01 has no priced parts: its material and process are unassigned, and its fabrication cost depends on those choices. Plausible desktop-printing material for the three parts would be a few tens of grams; that is an estimate, not a quote.

## Why there is no total

The baskets above cover different scopes and currencies, several items are sold out or pre-order, two CCW motors and most structural and support items are unpriced, and none of the quotes is a historical purchase. A single "build cost" would therefore be wrong.
