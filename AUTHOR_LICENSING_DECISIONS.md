# Licensing decisions (recorded)

Identical in both repositories. Replaces `AUTHORS_LICENSING_APPROVAL_REQUIRED.md` (release candidate 0.2.0-rc1), whose options are reproduced below with the selected choice.

**Decision:** both authors, Aarav Jalan and Abhinav Patro, approved the scheme below for their original, jointly owned project materials and the newly developed reference designs and documentation. The approval was communicated in writing to the repository maintainer on **21 October 2026** and applied in release 1.0.0. It covers only material the authors own. It grants nothing in third-party material and is not an approval of any manuscript text.
